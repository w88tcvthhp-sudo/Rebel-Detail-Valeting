Ceramic coating

A ceramic coating is a liquid that bonds to the paint and cures into a hard, glossy, water-repelling layer. It makes the car easier to keep clean and protects the finish for far longer than a wax.

The coating Rebel currently uses gives up to 24 months of protection. {{ confirm("coating product name, price or from-price, and how long the job takes") }}

What a coating does

  • Water and dirt run off more easily, so the car stays cleaner for longer
  • Washing is quicker and gentler
  • Adds depth and gloss to well-prepared paint
  • Resists bird droppings, traffic film and UV for longer than wax or sealant

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't make the paint scratch-proof
  • It won't stop stone chips
  • It won't hide swirls. It locks in whatever is underneath, which is why paint is usually polished first.
  • It doesn't mean the car never needs washing

Looking after a coated car

  • Wash with a pH-neutral shampoo and a clean mitt
  • Avoid automatic brush car washes
  • Dry with a clean microfibre towel or air
