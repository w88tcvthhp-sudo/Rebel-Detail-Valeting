What polishing can improve

  • Swirl marks, often caused by washing with dirty mitts, brushes or automatic car washes
  • Haze and dullness
  • Light scratches that sit within the clear coat
  • Marks left by previous poor polishing (holograms)
  • Light water-spot marks

What it can't fix

  • Scratches that have gone through to the colour coat or primer
  • Stone chips
  • Dents
  • Peeling or failed lacquer

These need paint repair, not polishing.

Enhancement or correction?

A one-stage polish (often called an enhancement) improves gloss and removes a good share of light defects in a day.

A multi-stage correction uses a cutting stage followed by a finishing stage. It removes more, takes longer and suits paint with heavier swirls.
