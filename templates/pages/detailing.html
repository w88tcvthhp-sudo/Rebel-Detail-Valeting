Signs your car would benefit from detailing

  • Fine circular scratches (swirl marks) show up in sunlight or under street lights
  • The paint looks flat or hazy even straight after a wash
  • Light scratches catch the light but don't catch your fingernail
  • You've just bought a car and want to protect the paint from the start

If your fingernail catches in a scratch, it has probably gone through the clear coat. Polishing can soften how it looks but won't remove it.
